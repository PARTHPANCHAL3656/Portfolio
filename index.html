§ 01 · Profile

My Story.

The world isn't waiting for anyone to be ready. I never was either. While most of my peers were still adjusting to college life, I was shipping products — real ones, with real users, zero budget, zero team.

I'm a Gujarati builder at the intersection of frontend development, AI engineering, and prompt architecture. The drive to build runs deeper than any course syllabus ever could. Being Gujarati comes with a certain default setting: if there's a problem, you solve it. If there's a gap, you fill it.

"I became a builder instead of becoming a user."

Anti-gatekeeping by default. If I figured something out, I share it — no preamble, no padding, just the raw thing. That's how ecosystems actually grow.

After school, I worked on myself too: became an extrovert, prioritized communication, read books, hit the gym. Soft skills aren't secondary — they're the multiplier. The degree is context, not the destination. The real curriculum is what I ship between semesters.

Three Rules I Live By

Work on networking and communication consistently — not just when convenient.

Stay open to feedback, debate, and honest opinions. That's how you actually grow.

MaxPrompt

A gamified AI literacy and prompt engineering platform. Users level up by solving real prompting challenges, earn Canvas API achievement certificates, and progress through a 7-day system that teaches them to think in prompts — not just read about it. Engineered a Vercel serverless proxy to keep API keys secure. Integrated OpenRouter for model flexibility. Zero frameworks — pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JS, with Vercel Analytics & Supabase integration for user database.
